💬 WhatsApp Command Center

Once a caregiver has NOTIFICATIONS or PAY_ON_BEHALF access, they can manage refills via a simple SMS reply:

CONFIRM Approve and pay for the pending refill order
SKIP Skip this month's refill cycle
SNOOZE Delay the refill by 7 days (or SNOOZE 14 for 14 days)
STATUS Get a summary of current subscriptions and pending orders
